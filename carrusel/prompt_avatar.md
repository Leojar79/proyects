# Prompt para el avatar de la portada

Úsalo en Midjourney, ChatGPT Images, Gemini, Leonardo, Ideogram, etc.
**Adjunta tu foto como referencia de rostro** (ideal: 2-3 fotos, de frente y bien iluminadas).

## Prompt principal (estilo 3D tipo Pixar, el más cercano a tu diseño actual)

```
Professional 3D stylized avatar of the man in the reference photo, Pixar-style
render, faithful likeness: short dark neatly-styled hair, short groomed stubble
beard, warm friendly smile, medium-tan skin, athletic build. He wears a black
polo shirt. He is seated at a modern desk working on a silver laptop whose back
faces the viewer, with a glowing soft blue screen light on his face. Three
floating glossy rounded-square app icons hover in front of him: an orange
starburst, a colorful ribbon, and a green knot. Clean light-blue gradient
studio background with soft bokeh, soft cinematic lighting, high detail,
confident and approachable expert look, upper body three-quarter view, square
1:1, subject on the right side with empty space on the left for text.
```

## Negative prompt (si la herramienta lo permite)

```
realistic photo, uncanny face, distorted hands, extra fingers, glasses,
text, watermark, logo text, messy background, cropped head
```

## Variantes de pose (cambia solo la frase de acción)

- **Señalando:** "pointing with his right hand toward three floating app icons, smiling at the camera"
- **Con tablet:** "holding a tablet showing a comparison chart, standing, looking at the camera"
- **Presentando:** "arms open presenting three holographic chat bubbles in front of him"

## Otros estilos (cambia la primera línea)

- **Ilustración plana corporativa:** "Flat vector corporate illustration, clean shapes, subtle gradients"
- **Semirrealista:** "Semi-realistic digital illustration, soft studio lighting"
- **Caricatura premium:** "Premium stylized caricature, slightly larger head, expressive"

## Después

Pásame la imagen generada (idealmente PNG con fondo transparente, o fondo liso) y la
integro en la portada con el mismo diseño, a 1254×1254.
